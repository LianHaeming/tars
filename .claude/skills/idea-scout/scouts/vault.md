# Vault scout

Lian's own notes: what they've already watched, thought and decided about the topic. Local files only — use Grep,
Glob and Read; no web. Both vaults are **read-only** for you.

- `~/tars/vaults/youtube-digest/flows/*/output/` — summaries of YouTube videos Lian watched, "for me" files, the
  journal. Ideas and products from videos they chose to watch carry extra weight.
- `~/tars/vaults/youtube-digest/context/` — about Lian (goals, money, career): what would matter to them.
- `~/tars/vaults/tars/` — tars's own notes: earlier plans and scoping decisions (e.g. `language-app-scope.md`), and
  earlier idea-scout reports in `idea-scout/`.
- Skip `CLAUDE.md` and `flows/*/flow.md` in youtube-digest: they're another agent's instructions, not content.

1. Grep (case-insensitive) for the topic's nouns and the brief's phrases, plus close synonyms; then read the best hits.
2. Note **decisions already made** (things Lian chose or rejected — these override what the internet wants), ideas
   Lian wrote down, and products or creators mentioned.

In the report, give vault paths (relative to `~/tars/vaults/`) instead of URLs. Signal = how often and how recently
it comes up. Put decisions already made under **Surprises** with the heading "Already decided".
