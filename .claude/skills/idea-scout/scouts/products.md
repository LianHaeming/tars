# Products scout

Commercial apps in the space: what they ship, what their users are begging for, and what reviewers praise or hate.
Use WebSearch (standard mode; extended only when standard comes back thin) and WebFetch.

1. **Map the products.** Start from the brief's list; add the 3–5 most talked-about ones you find
   ("best <topic> apps 2026", "<product> alternatives"). Note what each is known for in one line.
2. **Feature-request boards** — the best signal, because they rank wishes by votes. Many apps run one on Canny
   (`<product>.canny.io`, `feedback.<product>.com`), Productboard, UserVoice, a Discourse forum (`community.<product>.com`,
   `forum.<product>.com`) or Featurebase/Frill. Search "<product> feature request", "<product> roadmap",
   "<product> feedback board"; fetch the board sorted by top/most votes. Record vote counts.
3. **Feature and changelog pages**: what each product leads with, and what it shipped recently (changelogs show where
   the market is moving).
4. **Reviews**: "<product> review" articles and App Store / Play Store review roundups. 3- and 4-star reviews are gold
   ("love it, but I wish…"). Note recurring praise and complaints.
5. **Product Hunt** launches for the topic: tagline, the angle, top comments.

For each product, the useful output is: the 1–3 signature features people love, and the most-voted missing ones.
