# Web scout

Long-form thinking and personal builds outside the forums: blog posts, newsletters, dev write-ups, design case studies
and research. Use WebSearch (standard mode; extended when standard is thin) and WebFetch.

1. **Personal builds**: "I built my own <topic> app/dashboard/system", "my <topic> setup", "how I track <topic>" —
   dev.to, Medium, Substack, personal blogs, Notion/Obsidian template write-ups, spreadsheet templates people share.
   These are the closest thing to tars: one person building for themselves.
2. **Design and UX**: "<topic> app UX case study", "<topic> dashboard design", Dribbble/Behance concept titles,
   teardowns. What views and interactions do designers lead with, and why?
3. **Expert or research angle**: what the field says actually works (behavioural finance on spending awareness,
   second-language acquisition on spaced repetition, habit research…). Look for named methods and principles that
   could become features, with the source.
4. **YouTube**: video titles and descriptions only ("my <topic> system", "<topic> app tour") — don't try to watch.

Prefer specific, concrete ideas ("a calendar heatmap of no-spend days") over generic advice ("track your spending").
